- **ONE LOG FILE PER MODULE PER DAY, not one per qrexec call** (2026-10-07). Measured on a German 25H2 guest after one install and three boots: **386** log files in the QWT log directory, **368** of them `qrexec-wrapper-*.log`. The sweep that feeds the release gate caps at 60 files by priority, the wrapper logs won that contest, and the file it skipped was `qwt-deaths.log` - the record of the death the owner saw on his screen. `patches/windows-utils-one-log-per-module.patch` renames to `<module>-<date>.log`, opens `FILE_APPEND_DATA` (without `FILE_WRITE_DATA`, which is what makes a write atomic against the other writers) with `FILE_SHARE_WRITE`, and composes each line into ONE `WriteFile` - three calls would let two processes interleave inside a line, which is why an earlier append patch scored `append_is_safe` 0.37 and was never shipped. The date stays in the name because `PurgeOldLogs` deletes by `ftCreationTime`: a dateless name keeps its first creation time for ever and the retention sweep would delete the LIVE log. Jev: `sink` 0.79 (conf 0.72), `append_atomic_single_write` 0.84, `earlier_answer_superseded` 0.72; the event-log option, chosen at 0.82 earlier the same day, came back **0.01** once the brief had the three facts it lacked (a shared size-capped OS log at one RPC per line; ETW keeping nothing without a session; the three-write line). `docs/ADR-logging.md` records that reversal and the two numbers that do NOT favour the decision - `meets_owner_constraints` preferred the event log at 0.61 (conf 0.48) because a bespoke logger survives, and `missing_measurement = total-line-volume-per-boot` at **1.00**. Three defects found in review and fixed: a BOM race (two simultaneous starters both writing one; `CREATE_NEW` decides it now), the pid being LOST (it lived in the file name and nowhere else - the prefix is `pid:tid` and the analyzer takes both shapes), and **safe flush becoming a silent no-op** (`FlushFileBuffers` wants `GENERIC_WRITE` an append-only handle does not have, and its result was ignored; the log is opened `FILE_FLAG_WRITE_THROUGH` and a refusal is reported once). Guarded offline by `tools/tests/one-log-per-module-selftest.sh` (18 checks). **NOT MEASURED: nothing has run on a guest, so the file-count improvement is a PREDICTION**, and atomicity rests on the documented `FILE_APPEND_DATA` contract rather than a concurrency test of ours. [verified 2026-10-07]
