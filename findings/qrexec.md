- **A CALLER THAT DIES WHILE A CALL'S OUTPUT IS BACKED UP HANGS THE GUEST'S qrexec-wrapper INDEFINITELY - reproduced on the accepted ctrl-close package's wrapper, removed by core-agent 1df5a7b (branch stall/pump-close), 2026-09-30.** MECHANISM (from the code; the thread states confirm it): windows-utils' `VchanSendBuffer` waits for ring room with `while (space < size) Sleep(1)` and never checks that the peer is still there, so an output pump waiting there when the caller dies spins forever HOLDING `g_VchanCs`; if the child has exited, the wrapper's post-exit drain waits the 120 s bound for that pump, then the exit-code send blocks on the lock for good. Stock QWT has the same unguarded wait (its drain waited 1 s, then blocked the same way). Found by the pre-commit Jev review of the v4 change (owner rule), which also showed that since the always-close (54999a1) the cleanup closed and freed the vchan WITHOUT the lock while a pump could still be using it (a use-after-unmap on the caller-hung-up path; 0 crashes in 12 such trials, 6 per side - expected rare: the process usually exits before the sleeping pump wakes). THE FIX (reviewed by Jev before commit, eight rounds): the wait for room lives in the wrapper, under the lock, for the whole message, and ends when the peer is gone or cleanup is closing (a flag); the pumps read the vchan pointer under the lock; the close and the NULLing happen under it; the state is freed only when both pumps have exited. REPRODUCER (`scratchpad/hangup-repro.sh`; one Windows 11 24H2 subject from the accepted package's installed park; ONLY the wrapper file differs, swapped by hash, phases interleaved - swapped binaries, the package-level confirmation is the acceptance run): stream 64 MiB into a caller that stops reading after 1 MiB, kill the wrapper's child, kill the caller.
