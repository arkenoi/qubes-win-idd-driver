- P2 [OPEN; defect [verified 2026-10-28] in the source, fix committed 2026-10-28 but UNVERIFIED - unbuilt and unrun on a guest] GRANTIRPONMAIN: the gui-agent issued by-design-pending grant IRPs from the MAIN thread, which is also the thread that makes the synchronous evtchn close. The vendored xeniface header says of IOCTL_XENIFACE_GNTTAB_GRANT_PERMIT_FOREIGN_ACCESS_V2, verbatim: "This IOCTL must be asynchronous. The driver doesn't complete the request until the grant is explicitly revoked or the calling thread terminates" - so the granting thread carries a pending IRP for the grant's whole life. A core from a wedged gui-agent found ONE thread holding exactly that IRP (0x00222093) together with a synchronous IOCTL_XENIFACE_EVTCHN_CLOSE (0x00222048) that never returned. READ IN THE SOURCE 2026-10-28, that thread is the MAIN thread and there are TWO such sites, not one: perwindow.c PwSlabAcquire grants per-window slabs via PwAttachWindow (main.c:6824, main.c:9773) and PwSlabRelease deliberately NEVER revokes them ("keeping the grant is the entire point"), so those IRPs are pinned to main for the life of the process; and capture.c CaptureStagingGrantNow / StagingEnsure grant the staging buffer from main.c:8401. The capture thread's own framebuffer grant (capture.c GetFrame) is NOT implicated - main.c:15995 joins that thread first.
