- DRIVERVER TRAP: stampinf writes BUILD TIME (HH.MM.SS.ms) as the INF version; versions with leading fields in the WDDM encoding space (21.x, 23.x) make the graphics stack treat the driver as WDDM-2.x-era — registry modes never surface and the QIDD ioctl answers STATUS_OPERATION_IN_PROGRESS.
